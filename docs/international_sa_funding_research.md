# International Student Enrollment and S&A Funding Research

**Institution:** Seattle Central College  
**Research period:** 2023–24 through 2025–26  
**Status:** Preliminary findings — additional accounting verification required

## 1. Research Objective

This investigation examines how international student enrollment relates to Services and Activities (S&A) funding at Seattle Central College.

The central research question is:

**Do International Contract students contribute financially to Seattle Central's S&A budget, either through standard fee collections, transfers, or separate contributions?**

## 2. International Enrollment Findings

Data collected from the SBCTC Enrollment Data Dashboard:

| Academic Year | Total Headcount | International | International Contract |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2023–24 | 12,088 | 1,058 | 911 |
| 2024–25 | 12,378 | 1,132 | 994 |
| 2025–26* | 11,672 | 1,035 | 927 |

*2025–26 figures are treated as provisional because SBCTC warns that final data may not yet be available for all quarters.*

International students represented approximately 9% of total annual enrollment throughout the research period.

Most students identified by the broader International program filter also appeared under Contract funding. However, International and International Contract are distinct reporting classifications.

A difference of 4, 4, and 3 students was observed between the International + Contract funding filter and the International Contract program filter across the three years. The exact reporting explanation remains unverified.

These filtered populations must not be assumed to be mutually exclusive.

## 3. S&A and International Contract Accounting

### Fund 522 — Associated Students

Fund 522 accounts for approved student-activity revenues and expenditures.

SBCTC's standard S&A tuition distribution directs:

- 96.5% to Fund 522.
- 3.5% to Fund 860 for institutional financial assistance.

### Fund 146 — Contracts

International Contract students generally pay a fee in lieu of tuition.

SBCTC requires International Contract revenue to be recorded under:

- **Fund:** 146
- **Revenue account:** 4021065
- **SBCTC remittance:** 2.5% beginning FY2024

Fund 146 accounting does not establish whether Seattle Colleges subsequently transfers funds to S&A activities.

## 4. International Student Activity Fees

Seattle Central's April 2025 Board packet identifies an **International Activity Fee (`SB`)**.

Its published classification is:

- Category: Miscellaneous
- Basis: Incidental
- Amount: Variable

The fee also appears in Seattle Central's published fee schedule.

However, the available documentation does not establish:

- Which students are assessed the fee.
- How frequently it is charged.
- How much revenue was collected.
- Its general-ledger destination.
- Whether it contributes to Fund 522.

Additional international fees are documented separately in `international_fee_reference.csv`.

## 5. Historical Policy Discussions

Seattle Central meeting records from 2021–2024 describe discussions concerning S&A fee collection from international and Running Start students.

These records establish that the issue was considered historically.

They do not independently establish that a new fee policy was subsequently approved or implemented.

Public Board and financial materials reviewed so far have not resolved that question.

## 6. Current Research Conclusion

**Confirmed:** International Contract revenue is subject to different initial accounting treatment from standard state-funded tuition and S&A collections.

**Not confirmed:** Whether Seattle Central's S&A budget receives International Contract funds through direct charges, transfers, reimbursements, subsidies, or other contributions.

The absence of a documented transfer in the reviewed public sources is not proof that no transfer occurred.

No estimate of international students' total S&A contribution is presented because the available enrollment and fee-schedule data do not establish actual revenue collections.

## 7. Records Needed for Final Verification

The remaining investigation requires:

1. Approved fee-assessment rules applicable to International Contract students.
2. Seattle Central's ctcLink Item Type and general-ledger mapping for the International Activity Fee (`SB`).
3. International Activity Fee collections for FY2024–FY2026.
4. Seattle Central Fund 522 actual revenue reports.
5. Fund 146 transfers, reimbursements, or support payments involving student activities.
6. Relevant internal agreements or approved Board policy changes.

The Seattle Central Business Office, International Programs, and Student Leadership may hold these records.

## 8. Implications for the S&A Budget Explorer

The website should explain Fund 522 and Fund 146 using accessible language.

Published S&A fee rates should not be interpreted as proof that every student contributes the corresponding amount to Fund 522.

Until the outstanding accounting questions are verified, the website should not claim that International Contract students either contribute or do not contribute to Seattle Central's S&A budget.

This research remains supporting methodology rather than a confirmed dashboard finding.
