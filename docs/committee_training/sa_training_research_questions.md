# S&A Committee Training — Research Notes and Questions

**Project:** Seattle Central S&A Budget Explorer  
**Purpose:** Personal preparation for S&A Fee Committee training; questions to follow up on, not conclusions for the public website.  
**Research period:** AY 2023–24 through AY 2025–26  
**Status:** Open research; revisit after training  
**Last prepared:** October 2026

## 1. Quick refresher (read before training)

**The central question:** Do students enrolled under **International Contract** contribute to Seattle Central's Services and Activities (S&A) budget, either through an actual S&A charge, a transfer, a reimbursement, or other support?

### What we established

- **Fund 522 — Associated Students:** Accounts for approved student-activity revenues and expenditures. SBCTC's standard S&A fee accounting guidance directs **96.5% of the S&A tuition component to Fund 522**, with **3.5% directed to Fund 860** for institutional financial assistance. This describes *standard S&A fee accounting*, not necessarily the treatment of International Contract students.
- **Fund 146 — Contracts:** International Contract payments are generally *fees in lieu of tuition*, initially recorded under **Fund 146** and **revenue account 4021065**. SBCTC guidance calls for a **2.5% International Contract revenue remittance** starting FY2024. That remittance is **not** evidence of a payment to S&A.
- **Different questions:** A *tuition-rate component*, a *charge on a student's bill*, a *general-ledger credit*, an *interfund transfer*, and a *program contribution* are not interchangeable. Where money is first coded does not show every later transaction.
- **Fee-code lead:** Seattle Central lists **`SB: Int’l Activity Fee`** as **MISC / incidental / variable** in the April 2025 Board fee packet (p. 69). The available listings do **not** prove who pays it, how often it is charged, actual collections, or its accounting destination. `SB` is a **fee code**, not a fund number. A separate **International Student Services Fee** was listed at **$200/quarter**; do not conflate the two fees.
- **Historical context:** Seattle Central meeting records in **2021, 2023, and 2024** discussed international/Running Start S&A treatment, historical noncollection, possible support from International Programs, and potential changes. Those discussions are **not proof of a current adopted policy**. Board approval of an **S&A budget** is also not necessarily Board approval of a **new student fee**.
- **Evidence limit:** Public policy and financial statements reviewed so far have **not confirmed or disproved** a Seattle Central Fund **146 → 522** transfer, an alternative contribution, or an adopted assessment change. Districtwide audited financial statement summaries do not substitute for college-level ledgers, item-type mappings, and transfer records.

### Enrollment findings (SBCTC dashboard, Seattle Central/SVI)

| Academic year | All-source student headcount | International selected program | International Contract selected program | International + State filter | International + Contract filter |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2023–24 | 12,088 | 1,058 | 911 | 32 | 907 |
| 2024–25 | 12,378 | 1,132 | 994 | 26 | 990 |
| 2025–26* | 11,672 | 1,035 | 927 | 11 | 924 |

*2025–26 was marked provisional because the dashboard warned recent-year quarterly data may not yet be final.* International students were approximately **9%** of total annual headcount. **International (`SINT`) and International Contract (`SINC`) are distinct reporting flags**; filters are not established as mutually exclusive. There are differences of **4, 4, and 3** between the International Contract group and the broader International + Contract filtered counts. **Do not** label simple differences as “state-funded international students” or calculate revenue by multiplying headcount by a tuition fee.

## 2. What to listen for during training

- How the committee estimates **annual S&A revenue** versus deciding **allocations** and reporting **actual spending**.
- Who handles student fee **exemptions**, account **coding**, revenue reports, carryforwards, and reconciliations.
- Whether international/contract students, Running Start, and other populations are treated differently for S&A purposes.
- How contributions to student programming outside Fund 522 are recorded or recognized.
- Whether the training materials identify a contact for **Business Operations, Student Financials, International Programs, or Student Leadership**.
- Any mention of **actual S&A receipts** versus published tuition rates or budget authorizations.

## 3. Specific questions to answer (priority order)

### Priority A — Assessment and policy

1. **Are Seattle Central International Contract students currently assessed the standard S&A fee?** If not, what is the applicable exemption or fee-in-lieu policy, and where is the approved rule documented?
2. **Was a new international/Running Start S&A assessment ever formally approved** after the 2021–2024 discussions? If so, when did it take effect, what groups does it cover, and which Board action or approved fee schedule establishes it?

### Priority B — Money flow and program support

3. **Does any International Contract revenue (Fund 146) move into Fund 522**, directly or through transfers? If yes, under which authorization, transaction/account codes, and in what amounts for FY2024–FY2026?
4. **If there is no transfer to Fund 522, does International Programs still fund student activities indirectly?** Look for direct program payments, subsidies, reimbursements, cost-sharing agreements, or services funded outside the S&A budget. Which account or program holds these expenditures?
5. **How much actual S&A revenue did Seattle Central receive in each fiscal year (FY2024–FY2026)?** Ask for Fund 522 receipts by revenue category and whether estimates, allocations, actual collections, and expenditure totals are reported separately.

### Priority C — International Activity Fee (`SB`)

6. **Who is charged `SB: Int’l Activity Fee` and under what conditions?** Is it optional, incidental, program-specific, per event, or otherwise? Was it actually assessed in FY2024–FY2026, and what were the annual amounts collected?
7. **What is the ctcLink Item Type and GL configuration for `SB`?** Request the applicable **Fund, Class, Account, and Department** coding and, if applicable, the expense-side destination. The public label alone cannot answer this.
8. **Is `SB` related to standard S&A, or a separate funding source for international activities?** If separate, how are approved activities and spending reported?

### Priority D — Access and documentation

9. **Who can verify these answers?** Ask which records can be shared or publicly summarized: approved fee schedule/policy, ctcLink item-type mapping, Fund 522 revenue report, Fund 146 interfund transfers, and International Programs activity expenditures.
10. **How should these distinctions be explained to students** without implying that every published S&A-like tuition component is actually collected in Fund 522?

## 4. Short rehearsal before asking

> “I've been reviewing the S&A allocation memos alongside SBCTC tuition and accounting guidance for a student research project. My understanding is that standard S&A revenue is recorded primarily in Fund 522, while International Contract fee-in-lieu revenue initially goes to Fund 146. I also found an International Activity Fee listed separately in the college fee schedule, but couldn't establish its actual assessment or destination from public records. During training, I'd love to clarify whether and how International Contract students support S&A-funded activities, and which college records best explain that flow.”

Start with the **committee's normal funding process**. If the topic comes up naturally, ask **Question 1**, followed by **3 or 4**. Save detailed ledger and Item Type questions for the person responsible for accounting. Do not frame the question as an accusation or assume a transfer or exemption has been proved.

## 5. Records to seek (if authorized and available)

| Record | What it would tell us |
|---|---|
| Approved fee schedule / Board action | Actual authority, eligibility, and effective date for an S&A assessment |
| ctcLink `SB` fee Item Type configuration | Where fee assessments/receipts post in the general ledger |
| Seattle Central Fund 522 revenue detail, FY2024–FY2026 | Actual S&A receipts and their categories |
| Fund 146 transfer / journal summaries | Any International Contract-to-S&A transfers |
| International Programs support agreements and program expenditures | Direct or indirect student-activity support outside Fund 522 |
| S&A revenue estimates, allocation decisions, and expenditure reports | How collections differ from budgets and spending |

**Record-handling note:** Only obtain and share reports you are authorized to access. Avoid exporting student-level billing records or sensitive transaction details into a public GitHub repo. Summarize appropriately shareable, non-sensitive findings instead.
