# Understanding S&A Funding: Fund 522 vs. Fund 146

**Status:** Research draft — not yet approved for website publication.

## What is Fund 522?

Fund 522, officially called **Associated Students**, is used to account for revenue and expenditures associated with approved student activities at Washington community and technical colleges.

Under SBCTC accounting rules, standard Services and Activities (S&A) tuition revenue is divided as follows:

- **96.5%:** Fund 522 — S&A activities
- **3.5%:** Fund 860 — Institutional financial assistance

This fund is directly relevant to Seattle Central's S&A budget allocations.

## What is Fund 146?

Fund 146, officially called **Contracts**, accounts for contract-funded educational programs.

Examples include:

- International Contract
- Running Start
- Other qualifying contract programs

SBCTC requires International Contract revenue to be recorded under Fund 146, using revenue account **4021065**.

International Contract students pay a *fee in lieu of tuition*, rather than having their entire payment distributed according to the standard state-funded tuition structure.

## Why does the distinction matter?

The published nonresident tuition schedule includes an S&A fee component. However, this does not establish that the equivalent amount paid by an International Contract student is deposited into Fund 522.

Fund classification describes where revenue is initially recorded, but it does not necessarily reveal all subsequent transfers or expenditures.
